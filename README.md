## Projects

### [Sila](https://github.com/mohanad747/Sila)
<img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mohanad747/Sila/main/docs/sila.gif" width="360" align="right" alt="Sila's screen">

A virtual pet that lives on an M5StickS3 keychain. It feeds on nearby Wi-Fi,
notices phones around it, hears claps and music, giggles when you poke it,
screams when it falls, counts your steps and falls asleep when you rock it.
There's even a tilt mini-game.
